1. Background & Introduction

The rapid development of deep learning in recent years has significantly impacted computer vision. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) allow autonomous extraction of intricate representations from large-scale medical data, eliminating reliance on handcrafted feature design. However, CNN effectiveness depends heavily on the optimization algorithm used during training.

2. Research Overview

This study examines the impact of different optimizers on the performance of multiple CNN architectures for classifying Peripheral Blood Smear (PBS) images into Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL) states.

4. Temuan & Hasil Utama

Kinerja Tertinggi

Kombinasi model Xception dengan RMSprop berhasil mencapai akurasi tertinggi sebesar 99.69% dengan tingkat loss 0.0194 menggunakan pembagian data 80:10:10.

Anomali Performa

Meskipun mutakhir, ResNet50V2 justru menunjukan performa terlemah, membuktikan secara empiris bahwa kecocokan antara arsitektur dan optimizer matematis sangatlah vital.

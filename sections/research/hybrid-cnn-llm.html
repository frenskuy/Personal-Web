1. Background & Introduction

Plant diseases continue to threaten global agricultural productivity and food security. Conventional approaches to leaf disease identification—primarily visual examination by experts—are slow, labor-intensive, and susceptible to human error. There is an urgent need for an automated system to detect plant diseases early.

2. Research Overview

This study proposed a hybrid framework integrating a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) with a Large Language Model (LLM) to perform image-based plant leaf disease classification accompanied by interpretable diagnostic explanations.

4. Methodology & Architecture

Visual Backbone

EfficientNetV2-M was employed and trained on apple, grape, and potato leaf images from the PlantVillage dataset representing diverse visual disease patterns.

Interpretable Diagnostics

To overcome the black-box limitation of CNNs, prediction outputs from the visual models were transformed into structured prompts and parsed by the LLM to yield human-readable intervention steps.
